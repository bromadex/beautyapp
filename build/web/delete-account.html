Delete your BeauTap account

Last updated 8 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BeauTap and sign in.
  2. Go to Account settings. Clients: tap the person icon at the top of Home. Pros: tap Profile, then Account Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then Continue.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted straight away.

If you can't get into the app

Message BeauTap on WhatsApp at +263 78 377 8833. Send the email address or phone number on your account, and we'll delete it within 7 days. To make sure the request really comes from you, we may ask you to confirm from that email address or phone number.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo and contact details.
  • Your bookings, messages, chat photos, reviews and favourites.
  • For pros: your services, prices, work photos, certificates, products, location, salon membership and ID check photos.

What we keep

  • Records of payments made to BeauTap (amount, date and reference), with your name and account removed, for our accounts.
  • If your account was suspended for breaking our rules, we keep the record of the suspension and the reason, to protect other users.
