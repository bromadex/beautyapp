1. Who we are

BeauTap is an app and website that helps people in Zimbabwe find and book beauty professionals (hairdressers, braiders, barbers, nail and lash technicians, makeup artists, salons and others), and helps those professionals ("pros") manage their bookings. "We" and "BeauTap" mean the BeauTap service. This policy covers the BeauTap Android app and the website at beautyapp-swart.vercel.app.

2. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name, email address and password (stored scrambled, so we can't read it), or your name and email from Google or Apple if you sign in with them.
  • Your phone number, and whether you're a client or a pro.
  • A profile photo, if you add one.

If you're a pro

  • Your city, area and, if you add it, your street address and a map pin for where you work.
  • Your bio, services, prices, opening hours, work photos, certificates, products you advertise, and whether you're a woman or a man (so clients can filter, for example for massage).
  • The ways clients can pay you, such as your EcoCash number.
  • For the verified badge: a selfie and a photo of your ID. For business verification: your business name and registration number.

When you use BeauTap

  • Bookings: the service, date and time, where it happens, prices, notes, cancellations and no-shows.
  • Messages and photos you send in chat, reviews and ratings, and photos taken after a service.
  • Payment details you give us: transaction IDs and screenshots you upload as proof, and records of fees pros pay BeauTap.
  • Reports you make about a booking or a person, and the outcome.
  • Favourites, referral codes, and requests for BeauTap to come to your area.

From your device

  • Your location, only when you allow it and use a feature that needs it (see Location).
  • A notification token, so we can send you booking alerts, if you allow notifications.
  • Crash and error reports: what went wrong, which screen you were on, your phone type or browser, the app version and a random ID for your device. These don't include your messages or photos.
  • Settings saved on your device, such as your chosen city and light or dark mode.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and let you book, confirm, reschedule, cancel, pay for and review services.
  • To show clients the pros near them, and to work out distances and travel fees.
  • To send you booking reminders, messages and updates.
  • To keep BeauTap safe: checking IDs, handling reports, removing harmful content, and suspending accounts that break our rules.
  • To process plans, featured spots and product ads that pros buy.
  • To find and fix problems in the app, and to see which areas need more pros.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping payment records.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it to anyone.

4. Who can see it

Everyone, including people without an account

A pro's name, photo, bio, city and area, services, prices, work photos, ratings and reviews, products and verified badges. Reviews show the name of the person who wrote them.

The person you book with

  • A pro sees the client's name, phone number, booking details and, for home visits, the client's address.
  • A client sees the pro's contact details and, once the pro confirms the booking, the pro's street address if the appointment is at the pro's place.
  • You both see the messages and photos you send each other.

BeauTap staff

A small number of BeauTap admins can see account and booking information to support users, review ID checks, handle reports and stop abuse. ID photos are private and only admins can view them.

Companies that help us run BeauTap

  • Supabase stores our database and uploaded photos and handles sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the BeauTap website.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications.
  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Paynow processes online payments.

They only process information to provide their service to us. Some of them store data on servers outside Zimbabwe.

When you tap to open WhatsApp, Google Maps or your phone's dialler, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

When the law requires it

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or prevent fraud.

5. Payments

  • Clients usually pay pros directly, by EcoCash, another mobile wallet or cash. That money doesn't pass through BeauTap.
  • When a client marks a payment as sent, the transaction ID or screenshot they upload is shown only to that pro (and to BeauTap admins if there's a dispute).
  • When a pro pays BeauTap online, Paynow handles the payment. We receive the amount, the result and a reference, never your PIN or card number.
  • If a pro pays BeauTap by EcoCash directly, we keep the reference number they give us so we can match it.

6. Location

  • BeauTap only uses your phone's location after you allow it, and only when you use a feature that needs it: finding pros near you, working out travel fees, or a pro setting their map pin.
  • We don't track your location in the background.
  • A pro's city and area are public. Their map pin isn't shown to anyone. It's only used to show roughly how far the pro is from the city centre, and to work out travel fees.
  • You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings. You can still choose your city by hand.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, bookings, messages, reviews, photos and everything else linked to you. The exception is payment records: we keep the amount, date and reference, with your name and account removed, for our accounts.
  • Error reports are deleted automatically, at most 120 days after the error last happened.
  • If we suspend an account for breaking our rules, we keep the record of the suspension and the reason, to protect other users.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your details at any time in the app, under Account settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Account settings → Delete Account. If you can't get into the app, see how to delete your account.
  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us (see below).
  • Turn off notifications or location access in your phone's settings.

Under Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act, you can also complain to the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ), which oversees data protection.

9. Keeping it safe

Information sent between your device and BeauTap is encrypted. Access rules in our database mean people can only see what they're allowed to see. ID photos and payment screenshots are kept private. Passwords are stored scrambled. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and don't share it.

10. Children

BeauTap is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
